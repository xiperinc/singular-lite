FIXED · PROVEN

In-loop ceremony. Planners and critics went from half of all calls in August to 8 of 166 in September.

FIXED · EARLY SIGNAL

Lifecycle deadlocks. Four removed in 0.22.0, plus two supporting fixes in 0.23. Supervision per integration fell 22×. The result is not yet controlled.

OPEN · FIX PROPOSED

Five items. Two lifecycle deadlocks, the auditor’s first-try defect, retries into provider limits, worker clock sizing, and a supervision metric inside the engine.

HOW TO READ THIS

Both campaigns are field runs, not controlled experiments. The September campaign restarted 19 times on successive frozen runtimes and switched supervisor tools on 13–14 September. Codex ran on quota, so only Claude calls carry dollar amounts.

HOW TO CHECK IT

02 / Diagnosis one · August

The loop argued with itself.
0.21 made it stop.

In August the engine spent most of its calls deciding what to build. After 0.21.0, planning fell from half of all calls to one in twenty. Each integration took 17 calls instead of 142.

DIAGNOSIS · 27–28 AUG · AXON ON 0.20

855 provider calls produced 6 integrations, and planners and critics made 430 of them. The critic returned “revise” on 113 of 140 critiques. Every planned node parked when its revision budget ran out.

→

FIX · 5 SEP · RELEASE 0.21.0

Critic severities now have binding meanings, and the host downgrades a “revise” that names no blocking defect. Verification reruns only for risky work. Integration reuses proofs instead of rerunning the suite.

→

WHAT COUNTS AS LANDED

Output counts as landed when a commit from its attempt is an ancestor of the integration branch, whatever the runner reported. Adopted and successor work counts. Accepted work that was never merged does not.

THREE KINDS OF TOKEN

Output is what models wrote, and this report leads with it. Fresh input is new prompt material. Cached input is context re-read at a discount, 97% of engine input. It is never counted as work or money.

04 / Fixes and progress

After the lifecycle fixes, supervision
fell 22×. One clean run must prove why.

The four lifecycle fixes went live at 16:34Z on 14 September. In the 42 hours that followed, integrations per day more than doubled. Supervisor output per integration fell from 949k to 44k tokens.

Why this is a signal, not yet proof. The supervisor tool changed on 13–14 September, from Codex with 23 subagents to a single Claude session. The earlier week includes one-off rescue and planning work that the later window did not repeat. The later window covers 42 hours and 4 integrations, and it still needed hands: the supervisor finalized one dispatch record, adopted the TASK-1114 candidate and merged TASK-1115.
